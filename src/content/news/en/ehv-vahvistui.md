The suspicion in one horse was confirmed as EHV-1 on 22 April 2024. Gumböle Equestrian Centre stayed in quarantine and followed the authorities’ instructions.

Horses with signs were monitored, and the yard and the club updated clients as the Finnish Food Authority clarified its advice.
