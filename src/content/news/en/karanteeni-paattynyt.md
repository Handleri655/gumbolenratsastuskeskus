The voluntary quarantine that followed the herpesvirus infections at Gumböle Equestrian Centre ended on Monday 20 May 2024. The last horse with signs had a fever for the last time on Sunday 5 May 2024.

The yard and the club thanked everyone for their patience and for following the instructions. After the quarantine, visitors were welcome again.
