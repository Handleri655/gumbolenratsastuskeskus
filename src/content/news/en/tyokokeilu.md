Gumböle Equestrian Centre offers a work-trial place for someone who wants to test stable work in practice or clarify a career choice.

The trial is agreed with the Espoo employment services. It is not a job. The yard manager explains how the days are organised.

[Work trials on the City of Espoo website](https://www.espoo.fi/fi/palvelut/tyokokeilu)
